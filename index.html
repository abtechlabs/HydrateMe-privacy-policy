This Privacy Policy explains how AB TECH LABS (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information in connection with the HydrateMe Android application (the “App”). By installing and using the App, you agree to the practices described below. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

In short: HydrateMe works without an account. Your water intake, history and settings stay on your device. We do not run servers that collect your personal data. The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect certain device information as described in Section 3.

1. Information we handle

Information you enter in the App (stored locally)

  • Water intake entries (amount in ml and the time you logged it)
  • Your preferences: daily goal, default glass size, reminder interval and active hours, reminder on/off

This data is stored only in the App’s private storage on your device. We do not receive, access or store it on our servers. The App does not require you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details.

Information collected automatically by third-party advertising services

When ads are shown, Google AdMob may automatically collect certain information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and operating system information, app usage and ad interaction data. See Section 3.

2. How information is used

  • To show your daily progress and history inside the App.
  • To schedule and display local reminder notifications based on your settings.
  • To display ads, which helps us keep the App free.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for profiling or automated decision-making.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

HydrateMe uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial ads. AdMob is provided by Google LLC and may use identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, and to show personalized or non-personalized ads depending on your choices and region.

  • Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland), the App shows a consent form powered by Google’s User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. You can change your choice later where the App or your device makes this available.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

4. App permissions

PermissionWhy it is needed
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) To show your water reminders. You can deny or revoke this in system settings; the App will still work without reminders.
Internet & network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) Used only to load ads and the consent form. Your intake data is never uploaded by the App.

5. Data storage & deletion

Your intake history and settings are kept on your device. You can delete individual entries inside the App. To delete all App data, clear the App’s storage in Android Settings → Apps → HydrateMe → Storage or uninstall the App. Because we do not hold copies on our servers, we cannot recover data that has been deleted.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. Information may be processed by:

  • Google (AdMob / UMP) – to deliver ads and manage consent, under Google’s own privacy policy.
  • Legal authorities – only if required by applicable law.

7. Children’s privacy

HydrateMe is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an ad service connected to the App, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

8. Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Since we do not store your personal data on our servers, most of these can be exercised directly on your device (see Section 5). For data held by Google for advertising, you can manage your choices in your Google Ad Settings. You can also contact us at the address below with any request.

9. Security

Your data is stored in the App’s private sandbox on your device and is protected by Android’s security features. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. We recommend keeping your device protected with a screen lock and up-to-date software.

10. Health disclaimer

HydrateMe is a habit-tracking tool and does not provide medical advice. Your ideal water intake depends on your health, activity and climate. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for personal guidance.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
